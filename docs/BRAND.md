# Brand

**Company:** Turkey Vulture Software. **Public brand:** Turkey Vulture.
**Descriptor:** Software, Websites & Automation.

## Positioning and messaging

A small, independent consultancy with decades of hands-on engineering experience,
amplified by AI-assisted execution. AI is central to the workflow; human judgment
directs, reviews, and validates. Less repetitive implementation and conventional
agency overhead means more attention to practical, maintainable outcomes.

The hierarchy is: memorable promise → four capabilities → engineering advantage →
clear process/ownership → start a conversation. Website rescue is the entry point,
not the limit of the business. Give custom software, automation, and data/cloud
systems equal standing. Cost-conscious does not mean cheap.

`/how-we-build` carries the ownership-first argument in detail: client-owned
accounts, a small Cloudflare/GitHub stack, optional OpenRouter access for
applications that need AI, pragmatic exceptions for existing systems, and the
onboarding sequence. Keep provider choices as defaults, not requirements. Explain
AI-assisted engineering separately from AI features in a client application.

## Voice

Confident, capable, dryly funny, and approachable. The broken website or process
is the joke; the owner never is. Use one memorable line, then be useful. Explain
AI in terms of judgment, efficiency, and outcomes. Do not claim autonomous magic,
headcount, certifications, client history, awards, guarantees, or measured results.

Approved: “If your site stinks like death, we can help.” “Not every website needs
a funeral.” “Old-school engineering. AI-powered execution.” “Your website should
attract customers. Not scavengers.”

Avoid: “Your terrible website is embarrassing.” “Our award-winning team has helped
hundreds of clients.” “AI builds everything for pennies.” “Guaranteed 10× revenue.”
No cheapness claims, funeral-service aesthetic, or aggressive fear-based audit copy.

## Visual system

- Charcoal `#171917`: header, hero, approach, footer.
- Bone `#f1efe7`, paper `#f8f6f0`: reading surfaces.
- Rust `#b7442a`: buttons and marks on light surfaces.
- Pale rust `#e88c70`: emphasis on charcoal, focus outlines.
- Earth/sage `#dce0d2`: closing conversion band.
- Muted text `#62665e`; lines `#d7d8cc` on light surfaces.

Typography pairs a clean system grotesk (Arial/Helvetica) with Georgia italic
for a small number of expressive words. No remote font requests. Generous spacing,
thin rules, numbered editorial sections, square controls, and restrained icons.
Avoid generic agency card stacks, exaggerated gradients, and excessive rounding.

The hero depicts a turkey vulture, identified by its red bare head, pale hooked
beak, and dark layered feathers. The original bitmap is AI-created, not a claimed
wildlife photograph. See `ASSETS.md`. Use no eagles, ravens, skulls, Halloween
imagery, or photo hotlinks. The compact flight mark is an original SVG.

Animations are limited to subtle button hover movement and optional smooth anchor
scrolling. Reduced-motion preferences disable both. Mobile copy and usable controls
take priority over rigid desktop composition.
